  • My art style is naturally sketchy, so please don't expect a fully clean or polished finish
  • Product will be delivered digitally.
  • I reserve the right to refuse any commission request for any reason.
  • Work on your commission will begin after I send you the initial sketch and receive confirmation.
  • I do not accept commissions with deadlines. Completion usually takes 1-4 weeks after the sketch is started.
  • During holidays, progress may be slower, so please be patient.

F.A.Q

Do you make reference sheets?

Yes, I do offer reference sheets, u can check on the prices. Since they're not something I do regularly, please provide a plan or example image showing what you'd like included. We can then discuss pricing based on that.

When will you open commissions again?

I reopen commissions once my current queue is cleared, usually every 2–3 months.

How can I contact you?

You can reach me via Instagram DMs or by email at venuxiio@outlook.fr

When can I expect my commission to be completed?

As I work on multiple commissions at once, I'll keep you updated personally on your commission's progress.
